\section*{Problem Statement}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Problem Statement}
% Explain the joint travel/product-change planning problem, acknowledge related
% cleaning models, and introduce MPVRP-CC and the hybrid heuristic--CP method.

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Research question.} To what extent can a hybrid heuristic and
constraint programming approach solve MPVRP-CC effectively, and how does
accounting for changeover costs affect route planning and modeled operational
costs?
\end{quote}

\section*{Objectives}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Objectives}
The overall objective is to develop and assess a hybrid solution approach for
MPVRP-CC while examining the effect of changeover costs. The specific
objectives are to:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item define MPVRP-CC and formulate a mixed-integer linear programming
        reference model;
  \item develop a construction heuristic and a constraint programming model
        based on sequence variables;
  \item evaluate the hybrid method against the MILP baseline on synthetic
        instances; and
  \item analyze how including changeover costs changes plans and modeled costs.
\end{enumerate}
